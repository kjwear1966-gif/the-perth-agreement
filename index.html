The Perth Agreement

One Meal a Day. For Every Person.

A framework so no person goes hungry.

Every person alive today — and every child yet to be born — should have at least one meal every day.

Open for examination now.

Openness: If a better way to assure nutritious food emerges from independent examination, it replaces anything proposed here. The goal is the assurance. Not any single design.

The Path Forward

Simple idea. Simple plan.

One meal. Every person. Every day.

The Process

Three steps for examination and refinement.

1. Unite Leadership

Bring major faiths, Indigenous leaders, and community voices together to shape the early direction.

2. Academic Review

Universities and independent researchers test the assumptions, costs, and alternatives. Challenges are published with responses. Unresolved disagreements stay visible.

3. Invite Nations

Governments examine the strongest ideas that emerge and contribute their own positions.

Why This Matters

Not a finished system. A framework for examination, refinement, and responsible development.

One experimental idea: the Meal Standard

The Meal Standard proposes measuring value in nutritious meals. It is one idea among others. You can support the food-assurance principle without adopting the Meal Standard. Details sit in the resources.

What already works

Where is dependable access to nutritious food already working? What can others learn from it? Share proven practice alongside new proposals.

Status of the examination

Invitations go out in stages. Ideas that meet simple rules appear on the open record. Critiques and existing models are published as they arrive. Participation is not endorsement.
