The Perth Agreement

One Meal a Day. For Every Person.

A framework so no person goes hungry.

Every person alive today — and every child yet to be born — should have at least one meal every day.

Now open for global examination.

The Path Forward

Our Plan

A clear path for global examination and refinement

Unite Leadership

Why This Matters

It is not a finished system — it is a serious framework for global examination, refinement, and responsible development.

Where we stand today

What exists
The proposal, its supporting papers (including the Meal Standard, one proposed approach) and an open way for anyone to share ideas.

What is underway
Invitations to universities and student bodies, faith communities and all governments, and open examination of the proposal. Being invited is not the same as taking part, and taking part is not the same as endorsing.

What remains undecided
Funding, delivery and future governance. These are open questions for the research to explore.

Who looks after it today
The custodian, who can be contacted at contact@theperthagreement.com.

What taking part means

Taking part means helping to examine and develop The Perth Agreement. Your questions, criticism and ideas are welcome. You may see something that has been overlooked, or a better direction entirely.

The aim is one nutritious meal, every person, every day. How best to achieve it is open. The Meal Standard is one proposed approach, available for examination, improvement or replacement.

Every university, community, organisation and individual is welcome. You do not need an invitation or anyone’s endorsement to share ideas.
