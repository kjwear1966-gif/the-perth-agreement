The Perth Agreement

One Meal a Day. For Every Person.

A global framework to ensure no person goes hungry.

Every person alive today — and every child yet to be born — should have at least one meal every day.

Now open for global examination.

Openness principle: If independent examination produces a better way to assure nutritious food than any idea here, that better way should replace it. The goal is the assurance, not any particular design.

The Path Forward

Our Plan

A clear path for global examination and refinement

Unite Leadership

Bring together major faiths, Indigenous leaders, and community voices to help guide the early direction of the Perth Agreement.

Academic Review

Work with universities and independent researchers to test, refine, and strengthen the model. Thoughtful challenges will be published with any response and with disagreements that remain unresolved.

Invite Nations

Invite governments and nations to consider and support the strongest ideas that emerge from this collaborative process.

Why This Matters

It is not a finished system — it is a serious framework for global examination, refinement, and responsible development.

One experimental idea among others: the Meal Standard

The Meal Standard is a proposal to explore a unit of value measured in nutritious meals. It is one idea among others. You can examine or support the food-assurance principle without adopting the Meal Standard. Details are in the resources.

What already works

Where is dependable access to nutritious food already working well? What can others learn from it? Share accounts of proven practice alongside new proposals.

Status of the examination

Invitations are being sent in stages. Ideas that meet simple rules appear on the open record. Critiques and existing models will be published as they arrive. Participation is not endorsement.
