What Happens Next

The Perth Agreement asks one question: should every person on Earth, and every child yet to be born, be assured at least one nutritious meal every day?

An invitation to students around the world

Nearly 300 universities and student bodies are being invited in stages. Examine the proposal. Challenge its assumptions. Improve it or suggest a different direction. Agreement is not required.

How it works

  • Share an idea any time. If it meets the simple rules (be kind and give it a go), it goes on the open record.
  • Ideas stay open for others to read, challenge, and build on.
  • Building towards a proposed signing in August 2027. This is a suggestion. Better ideas are welcome.
