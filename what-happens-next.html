What Happens Next

Examine the proposal. Challenge its assumptions. Improve it or suggest a different direction. Agreement with the proposal is not a condition of taking part. Those taking part will have the opportunity, in time, to take on its leadership.

Every idea is open to all

Share an idea any time. If it meets the simple rules (be kind and give it a go), it goes on the open record straight away, for the world to read, challenge and build on.

How it works

  • Within about two months of the invitation: we'd love a first response, with questions, suggestions or what you'd like to explore.
  • Ideas grow as more universities join; every idea stays open to all.
  • Building towards a proposed signing of The Perth Agreement in August 2027. This is a suggestion. Better ideas are welcome.

An open invitation process

We publish who has been invited, when invitations were sent and the wording used. See the outreach record on the Student Circle.

Where we stand today

What exists
The proposal, its supporting papers (including the Meal Standard, one proposed approach) and an open way for anyone to share ideas.

What is underway
Invitations to universities and student bodies, faith communities and all governments, and open examination of the proposal. Being invited is not the same as taking part, and taking part is not the same as endorsing.

What remains undecided
Funding, delivery and future governance. These are open questions for the research to explore.

Who looks after it today
The custodian, who can be contacted at contact@theperthagreement.com. No organisation, government or university is claimed as a supporter, partner or endorser.
