How your idea is used

Share one question, one idea, one critique, or one account of what already works.

  1. Ideas that meet the simple rules (be kind, give it a go) appear on the open record straight away.
  2. Others can read them, comment, build on them, or critique them.
  3. You can continue developing your own idea.
  4. No idea is required to be adopted. The strongest ones surface through use and further examination.
  5. Unresolved disagreements stay visible. Challenges are published with any response.

Agreement with any proposal is not required to take part. The invitation is to examine, not to endorse.

Share at the Student Circle or by contact.
