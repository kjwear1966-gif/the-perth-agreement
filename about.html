About the Perth Agreement

The Perth Agreement is a simple idea:

Every person alive today — and every child yet to be born — should know they will have at least one meal every day.

I am planting the seeds of an idea. Once it grows, it belongs to those who inherit it.

At its heart is a basic recognition: one nutritious meal every day is dignity, not charity.

This is not a finished answer. It is a starting point.

Its purpose is discussion, research, examination, challenge, improvement, and participation.

Openness: If independent examination produces a better way to assure nutritious food than any idea here, that better way replaces it. The Foundational Custodian keeps the invitation honest and the process open. The role is not to protect any particular design.

No single person, organisation, nation, or generation owns its future. It must reflect a collective perspective of humanity.

Do we want this?

One possible pathway is to bring representatives from major faiths, truth-seeking traditions, universities, governments, communities, and future generations to examine and develop the framework further. This is only a suggested starting point.

My hope is that Aboriginal Elders may one day host an initial signing in Perth, where the conversation can formally begin. The world's oldest continuing culture hosting a declaration of intent for cooperation.

How the framework evolves is determined by those who inherit it.

Each participating tradition may nominate two young representatives to help guide early development. Any future council that emerges should make its own decisions. Ideally its representatives remain under 25 so the next generation has a direct voice.

They may draw on global thinkers and leaders where needed. The strongest outcome is the goal.

This is an invitation. Not a finished plan.

If the idea speaks to you through curiosity or conviction, you are already part of it.

Transparency as a Core Principle

Openness and accountability are required.

Every major decision and communication should be auditable and transparent.

Nothing is done in the shadows.

The generation here and now decides whether this is worthy of discussion, development, and support.

Your call.
